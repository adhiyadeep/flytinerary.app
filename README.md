## From “where should we go?” to a travel plan

Flytinerary helps travelers explore Europe trip options
and build an itinerary around the time and budget they have.

Whether you have a destination in mind or are still comparing
possibilities, start with the details that matter to your trip.

## How to plan your journey

1. **Enter your starting city** — choose where your journey begins.
2. **Set your budget and travel dates** — give your plan a practical starting point.
3. **Explore possible journeys** — compare destinations and routes.
4. **Review your itinerary** — use it as a starting point for your travel planning.
